# Screen Gain Simulator

**Use it here: https://screengainsim.github.io/**

Compare how your projector would look on two screens (gain, size, ALR, room light, seat position, SDR and HDR) using
your own meter readings. It runs in your browser; anything you enter stays in your browser.

It's a comparison tool, not a preview: it shows which screen is brighter, by how much, and how room light affects each
one. It won't look exactly like sitting in your room. The notes inside the app explain the limits.

The photo scenes are AI-generated stand-ins; your own movie screenshots are a better test.
